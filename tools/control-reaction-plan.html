Control Plan and Reaction Plan

Write a control plan with the characteristic, specification, measurement, sampling, control method and reaction plan for each key step, and check it is complete.

For each critical characteristic say what is controlled, how it is measured and sampled, what method keeps it in control and exactly what happens when it goes out. A reaction plan should contain a stop, contain or escalate action and an owner. Example: the hone cell after the improvement.
