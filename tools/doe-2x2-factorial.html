2×2 Factorial Experiment Analyzer

Enter the results of a two-factor, two-level experiment and get main effects, the interaction, significance and an interaction plot.

Run all four combinations, ideally with two or three replicates each, in random order. Enter the results of each run separated by commas. Replicates give the error estimate needed for p-values. Example: honing time and media grade against the chip rate in percent (lower is better).
