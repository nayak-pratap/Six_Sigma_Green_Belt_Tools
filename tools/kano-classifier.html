Kano Model Classifier

Classify features as must-be, performance, attractive or indifferent from the answers to a functional and a dysfunctional question.

For each feature ask two questions: “How would you feel if the product HAS this feature?” (functional) and “How would you feel if it does NOT?” (dysfunctional). Pick the typical answer for a customer group. Example: features of a carbide insert offering.

All calculators and toolsWorked examples are illustrative. Check your own data, standards and customer requirements before acting on a result.
