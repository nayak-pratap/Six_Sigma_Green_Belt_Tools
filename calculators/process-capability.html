Process Capability Calculator (Cp, Cpk)

Compare your process spread and centring with the specification limits and estimate parts per million out of spec.

Cp = (USL − LSL) ÷ 6σ. Cpk = min[(USL − mean) ÷ 3σ, (mean − LSL) ÷ 3σ]. Use the within-subgroup σ for Cp and Cpk and the overall σ for Pp and Ppk. Valid only for a stable, roughly normal process. Example: a bore of 10.0 mm ± 0.6 running slightly off-centre.
