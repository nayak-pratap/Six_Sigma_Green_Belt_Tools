Control Chart Limits Calculator

Centre line and 3σ limits for X-bar and R, I-MR, p, np and c charts, with the constants shown.

Limits sit at the centre line ± 3σ of the plotted statistic. A lower limit below zero is shown as zero. Use limits from a stable baseline period, then keep them fixed. Example: five-piece subgroups of a 50 mm dimension with an average range of 4.
