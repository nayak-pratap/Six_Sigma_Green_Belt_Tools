FMEA Risk Priority Board

Score failure modes for severity, occurrence and detection, see the RPN and sort by risk.

Score severity (S), occurrence (O) and detection (D) from 1 to 10, where 10 for detection means “very hard to detect”. RPN = S × O × D. Newer FMEA standards (AIAG and VDA, 2019) rank risk by Action Priority instead of raw RPN, because the same RPN can hide very different severities. High severity deserves attention whatever the RPN. Example rows come from a chipped-insert case.
