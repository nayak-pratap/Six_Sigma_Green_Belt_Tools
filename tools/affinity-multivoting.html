Brainstorming, Affinity Grouping and Multivoting

Capture ideas, group them under themes, record votes and see which themes the team rates highest.

Record one idea per row, give it a theme to group similar ideas, and enter the votes it received (for example each person gets 5 dots). Themes are ranked by total votes. Example: causes of chipped inserts after a team brainstorm.
