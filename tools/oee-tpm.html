OEE Calculator (Total Productive Maintenance)

Compute availability, performance, quality and OEE from a shift’s data and see the time lost to each of the big loss types.

Availability = run time ÷ planned time. Performance = (ideal cycle time × pieces) ÷ run time. Quality = good pieces ÷ total pieces. OEE = A × P × Q. World-class benchmark often quoted is 85% (90% × 95% × 99.9%). Example: one 450-minute shift on a hone machine.

All calculators and toolsWorked examples are illustrative. Check your own data, standards and customer requirements before acting on a result.
