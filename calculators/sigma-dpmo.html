Sigma Level and DPMO Calculator

Turn defect counts into DPU, DPMO, yield and sigma level, with or without the 1.5σ shift.

DPU = defects ÷ units. DPMO = defects ÷ (units × opportunities) × 1,000,000. Yield = 1 − defects ÷ (units × opportunities). Sigma = z(1 − DPMO/10⁶) + 1.5. Example: 12 defects on 500 inserts with 4 opportunities each.
