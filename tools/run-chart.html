Run Chart with Runs Test

Plot data in time order against its median and test for clustering, mixtures, shifts and trends.

Readings in time order. The test counts runs of points on the same side of the median. Too few runs suggest clustering or shifts; too many suggest mixing or oscillation. Example: 24 weekly chip rates where a shift occurs in the second half.
