5S Workplace Audit

Score a work area on sort, set in order, shine, standardise and sustain, and see which S is weakest.

Walk the area with the team and score each statement from 0 (not at all) to 4 (fully in place, everyone follows it). Be strict: a score of 4 means a stranger could see it. Example: a hone cell part-way through its 5S journey.
