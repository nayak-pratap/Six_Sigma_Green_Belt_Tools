5 Whys Root Cause Worksheet

Walk from a problem to a verified root cause, test the chain in reverse and write a countermeasure you can copy into a report.

Ask “why” until you reach a cause you can fix and whose removal would stop the problem. Base each answer on evidence, not opinion. Example: a batch of chipped inserts.

All calculators and toolsWorked examples are illustrative. Check your own data, standards and customer requirements before acting on a result.
