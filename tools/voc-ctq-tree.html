Voice of the Customer to CTQ

Turn what customers say into needs and then into measurable critical-to-quality requirements, and flag any that are still vague.

Write the customer’s own words first, then the need behind them, then a measurable requirement with a target. A CTQ without a number cannot be managed. Example: voices from machine shops using carbide inserts. The last row is deliberately vague.
