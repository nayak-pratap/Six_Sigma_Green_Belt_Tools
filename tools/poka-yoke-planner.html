Poka-Yoke (Mistake-Proofing) Planner

List error-prone steps, rate the risk and the type of control, and see where to add prevention instead of relying on inspection.

For each error that can happen, rate severity and how often it occurs (1 to 10), and say how it is controlled today. The strongest control makes the error impossible; the weakest just asks someone to look. Example: errors in the hone cell.

All calculators and toolsWorked examples are illustrative. Check your own data, standards and customer requirements before acting on a result.
