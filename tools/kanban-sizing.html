Kanban Card Sizing Calculator

Work out how many kanban cards a loop needs from demand, replenishment time, safety stock and container size.

Cards N = ⌈ D × L × (1 + s) ÷ C ⌉. The maximum stock in the loop is N × C. Lead time is the whole cycle from the card being released to the full container arriving. Example: replenishing hone media boxes for a cell using 120 units a day.

All calculators and toolsWorked examples are illustrative. Check your own data, standards and customer requirements before acting on a result.
