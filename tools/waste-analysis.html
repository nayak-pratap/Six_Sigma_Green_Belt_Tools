Value-Added and Waste Analysis

Classify each step’s time as value-added, necessary non-value-added or one of the wastes, and see where the time goes.

Walk the process and note the time each step takes, then classify it from the customer’s point of view: would they pay for it? Example: following one lot through the hone cell.
