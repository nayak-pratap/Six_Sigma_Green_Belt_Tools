Change Management Plan (ADKAR)

Score each affected group on awareness, desire, knowledge, ability and reinforcement, find their first barrier and plan the action.

For each group affected by the change, score each ADKAR element from 1 (not there) to 5 (fully there). The first low score, reading left to right, is that group’s barrier point and tells you what to do. Example: introducing new tray handling in the hone cell.
