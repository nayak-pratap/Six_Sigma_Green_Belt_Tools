Spaghetti Diagram and Travel Distance

Enter the route of a person or part on the floor plan, compare a current and a proposed layout, and see the distance and time saved.

One stop per line: x and y in metres from a corner of the floor, then a label. Stops are visited in order. Example: moving a part from stores through the hone cell to coating, before and after rearranging the cell.
