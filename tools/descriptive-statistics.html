Descriptive Statistics and Histogram

Paste measurements and get the summary statistics, a histogram with a normal curve, a normality hint and optional spec-limit performance.

Separate values with spaces, commas or new lines. Example: 40 bore diameters in mm from one shift. Enter spec limits to see Pp and Ppk, which use the overall standard deviation.

All calculators and toolsWorked examples are illustrative. Check your own data, standards and customer requirements before acting on a result.
