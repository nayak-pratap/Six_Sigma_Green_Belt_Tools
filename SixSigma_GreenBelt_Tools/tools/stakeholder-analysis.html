Stakeholder Analysis Grid

Rate influence, interest and support for each stakeholder, see them on a power-interest grid and find the biggest support gaps.

Influence and interest run from 1 (low) to 5 (high). Support runs from −2 (strongly against) to +2 (champion). Set where you need each person to be, and the gap shows where to spend your time. Example: a hone-cell improvement project.

All calculators and toolsWorked examples are illustrative. Check your own data, standards and customer requirements before acting on a result.
