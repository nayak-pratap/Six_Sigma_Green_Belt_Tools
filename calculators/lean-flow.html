Lean Flow Calculators

Takt time, Little’s law, process cycle efficiency and rolled throughput yield in one place.

Takt time

Takt = available time ÷ demand. A step slower than takt is a bottleneck.

Little’s law

WIP = throughput × lead time. To shorten lead time at the same throughput, cut WIP.

Process cycle efficiency

PCE = value-added time ÷ lead time. Under 10% is common before Lean work.

Rolled throughput yield

RTY = product of step yields. Three good-looking steps still lose almost 10% of units.
