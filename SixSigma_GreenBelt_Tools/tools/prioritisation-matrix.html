Weighted Prioritisation Matrix

Weight your criteria, score each option from 1 to 5, and rank the options by weighted score.

Give each criterion a weight from 1 to 10 (higher means it matters more), then score every option from 1 to 5, where 5 is best on that criterion. Phrase every criterion so that a higher score is better: write “Low cost”, not “Cost”. Example: choosing a fix for chipped inserts.
