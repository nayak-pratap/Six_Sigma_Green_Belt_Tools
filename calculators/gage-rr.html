Gage R&R Calculator

Judge a measurement system from its repeatability, reproducibility and part-to-part variation.

GRR = √(EV² + AV²). Total = √(GRR² + PV²). %GRR = GRR ÷ Total. Number of distinct categories = 1.41 × PV ÷ GRR (aim for 5 or more). Common guide: under 10% good, 10% to 30% conditional, over 30% unacceptable. Take the σ values from your ANOVA or average-and-range study output.
