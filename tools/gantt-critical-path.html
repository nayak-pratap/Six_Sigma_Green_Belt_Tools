Gantt Chart and Critical Path

List activities with durations and predecessors, and get the schedule, the float of each task, the critical path and a Gantt chart.

Give each activity a short ID, a duration in days, and the IDs of the activities that must finish first, separated by commas. Example: a 12-week Green Belt project.
