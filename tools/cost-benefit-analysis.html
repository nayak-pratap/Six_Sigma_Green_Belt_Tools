Cost-Benefit and Payback Calculator

Test whether an improvement pays back: payback period, NPV, IRR, ROI and benefit-cost ratio with a year-by-year view.

Use any currency. Savings should come from agreed numbers, such as scrap cost avoided or hours saved at a finance-approved rate. Example: a hone-media change that costs 85,000 to introduce and saves 40,000 a year.
