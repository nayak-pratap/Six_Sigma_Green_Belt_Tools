Scatter, Correlation and Regression

Paste pairs of x and y values, see the scatter plot with a fitted line, r, R², slope, p-value and a prediction.

One pair per line, x then y, separated by a space or comma. Example: cutting speed in m/min against flank wear in µm after a fixed cut. Correlation shows association, not cause, and a line is only trustworthy inside the range of your data.
