Sample Size Calculator

How many to measure: estimating a mean, estimating a proportion, or comparing two means, with finite-population correction.

Mean: n = (z·σ ÷ E)². Proportion: n = z²·p(1−p) ÷ E². Two means: n per group = 2(zα/2 + zβ)²σ² ÷ Δ². Finite population: n′ = n ÷ (1 + (n−1)/N). Example: estimating mean edge radius to ±2 µm when σ is about 5 µm.
