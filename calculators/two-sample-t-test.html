Two-Sample t-Test (Welch)

Test whether two process means differ, from summary statistics, with p-value and 95% confidence interval.

Welch’s test does not assume equal variances: t = (x̄₁ − x̄₂) ÷ √(s₁²/n₁ + s₂²/n₂), with Welch–Satterthwaite degrees of freedom. Two-sided p-value. Example: tool life in minutes for two coatings.
