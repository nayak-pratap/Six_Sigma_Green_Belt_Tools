Box Plot and One-Way ANOVA

Compare groups side by side with box plots and individual points, and test whether the group means differ.

One group per line: a name, a colon, then the values. Use this for the “where does the variation come from” look: by shift, machine, operator or supplier lot. Example: chip rate (%) by shift, 8 lots each.
